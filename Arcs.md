## The Garaztian war (third term)

A bailiff's war. Garaztia paid for its centralisation with loans from Arrosian merchant houses and can no
longer pay the interest. The Arrosian Republic, which fights only when it is owed money, sends troops to
seize the Garaztian customs houses and collect the debt at the border, much as Arrosia's own creditors once
took its crown's customs. Arrosia calls it a collection, Garaztia an invasion.

- **Foreshadowing, 1857:** the King of Garaztia visits Aurelle. He is charming and popular with the crowds,
  and privately asks for a loan to meet the interest.
- **Aurelia's stake:** Garaztia borders Provencie Gares. Arrosian troops would stand on Aurelia's doorstep,
  refugees would cross into Gares, and Gares has families of the old Garaztian border lords with kin across
  the border.
- **The players' choice:** pay Garaztia's debt and gain a grateful neighbour, stand aside, or quietly side
  with the creditors. The choice sets how friendly Arrosia is when the Meridia dispute begins.
- **Garaztia's valleys** still remember the charters the crown took from them. They may rally to the crown
  against the foreign creditors, or welcome the Arrosians, who offer to restore the charters in exchange
  for the customs. The GM decides, or the players' choices do. Aurelian politicians will see their own
  Anzarian charters in it.
- **The Union of Bon** watches nervously, since some of its princes owe Arrosian houses too.

## The colonial arc (from the second term)

Aurelia-Anzaria discovers how much it depends on other powers' colonies, and begins to want its own. Meridia
is the one continent the Mugi–Arrosia treaty leaves unclaimed, and Aurelia-Anzaria never signed that treaty.
Arrosia, looking to make up for Lurberria, wants Meridia too. Mugi is glad to see Arrosia challenged, but
wary of a colonial Aurelia-Anzaria.

**Second term: seeds.** A few cheap flavour events that never compete with the South Arpal arc.

1. **The Cotton Famine Petition** (if the tariff war makes cotton dear, for instance after the duty-for-duty
   answer): Iracterie mill owners ask the Assembly to look for cotton "beyond the Mugian sea", since
   Ukulwian cotton reaches them only through Mugi.
2. **The Voyage to Bhartunia:** a private Aurelian merchant venture reaches the Bhartunian coast and comes
   home with cotton samples and stories. The newspapers love it.
3. **The Admirals' Memorandum** (after the Squadron off Lanten, or the war): the Anzarian admirals ask for a
   coaling and supply station in the south. A fleet that can only defend its own harbours cannot protect
   Aurelian trade.
4. **An Arrosian Note** (around 1858): polite and slightly amused, the Republic reminds Aurelia-Anzaria that
   it "takes an interest in the southern trade".

How South Arpal ends sets how strong the push is:

| South Arpal ending | Colonial push |
| --- | --- |
| Treaty of Bon | Mugian cotton flows again; the push is weaker and comes from the admirals more than the mills. |
| Long tariff war, or war | Cotton is scarce; the mills drive the push hard, and the public backs it. |
| The Anzarian fleet sails and holds | The admirals grow bold; the station request becomes a demand. |

**Third term: the foothold.** While Arrosia is tied down in Garaztia, a bill charters an Aurelian-Anzarian
trading company, the Union's answer to the Iratic Company, and the first trading post or treaty is made in
Bhartunia. Arrosia can only protest.

**Fourth term: the dispute over Meridia** with Arrosia, and perhaps Mugi.
