# History

Aurelia, Mugi and the lands around them were once part of the **Bon Empire**, which survives today,
reformed, as the **Union of Bon**. The Empire held the western half of Bonaria and a colony across the sea in Ocidaria,
called **Ukulwia**. Anzaria, Garaztia and Arrosia were never part of it.

Aurelia won its independence in a revolt about five hundred years ago. The treaty between the revolting Aurelia and the Bon Empire, **the Severance of Carden**, signed in 1352 in the Arpal town that became North Arpal's capital, drew
the border through the Arpal country: the north became Aurelia's North Arpal, and the south stayed in the
Empire. Mugi stayed in the Empire longer than Aurelia did, and when it left, it took **South Arpal** with
it. The treaty under which it left also split the Ukulwian colony: Mugi took the northern coast, and the
Empire kept the rest. The Empire's share later broke free as **Nouterria**, **Calidia** and **New Bon**.
Mugi's share is still Mugian, and is the only land that still bears the name Ukulwia.

Losing Aurelia, then Mugi, then the colonies drained the Empire's princes, who had paid for every failed
war. They forced the Emperor to sign the **Act of Union**, which left him only the powers the Diet grants
and made every principality master of its own affairs. The Empire has been called the Union of Bon ever
since.

The name is a mistake from the first landing. When the Empire's first ships landed on the western coast,
the people there met them with spears. The Imperial officers asked by signs what the land was called, the
warriors answered *uq 'ulwa*, "we fight", and the officers wrote it down, butchered, as the land's name.
The colonies that broke free from Bon chose new names to be rid of it. Many Aurelians still loosely call
the whole continent Ukulwia, though maps and the learned say Ocidaria. The native Ukulwians have a feeling
for what the butchered name means.

Today **Mugi** and **Arrosia** are the two colonial powers, and a treaty between them divides the world
into spheres of influence: Mugi's over Ocidaria, Arrosia's over Orienzia. Lurberria's revolt against
Arrosia threw that balance off. The treaty says nothing of **Meridia**, and Aurelia-Anzaria never signed
it.

# The Crown

## Aurelian Line
* **King Orias II (Reigned 1741 - 1768):** Maintained Aurelia's economic and cultural height. His late
  reign was marked by careful diplomatic outreach to Anzaria to secure northern trade routes.
* **Queen Valerie Aureliene (Reigned 1768 - 1802):** Inherited the throne as sole ruler of Aurelia. Her
  strategic marriage to King Velzan III created the treaty foundation for the personal union, with their
  son designated to inherit both crowns. She gained the sobriquet Aureliene for her reforms and support of
  industrialization in Aurelia.

## Anzarian Line
* **King Arthan V (Reigned 1738 - 1765):** A formidable military leader who consolidated Anzaria's
  northern tribes and administrative structure, leaving a stable realm to his son.
* **King Velzan III (Reigned 1765 - 1798):** Co-ruled his native Anzaria during his marriage to Queen
  Valerie. His reign focused on navy modernization and securing noble approval for his son's dual
  inheritance.

## Personal Union
* **King Arthas I (Reigned 1802 - 1836):** Born *Arthan*, his name was stylized as *Arthas* upon
  coronation. He officially brought both crowns into personal union in 1802 following his mother's death
  (having already inherited Anzaria upon his father's death in 1798) and established a united army,
  currency and foreign policy for the two realms.
* **King Arthas II (Reigned 1836 - present):** Ascended 60 years after the marriage of Queen Valerie and
  King Velzan III. Known for his diplomatic skill both in Aurelian and Anzarian courts. The 1848 Aurelian
  riots forced him to ratify the *Aurelian Constitution* with universal male suffrage. A reform passed by
  the first Assembly extended the vote to women of property.

# The world

The known world has four continents, named here as Aurelians name them. On the map, sovereign states are
written upright and everything else in italic: dependencies, colonies and the uncharted lands of the
**Unnamed Tribes**, which are sovereign only because no power has charted them.

## Bonaria

The northern continent, the land of the old Empire.

- **Aurelia-Anzaria:** the Union of the two crowns, across the north of the continent. Aurelia lies in the
  south of the Union, with its capital at **Aurelle**. Anzaria lies in the north, with its capital at
  **Sulmar** on the Aurelian border; its other towns include **Adrinan**, also on the border, **Ucanzean**
  in the far west and **Istralan** on the north coast.
- **Mugi:** south-west of Aurelia. See below.
- **The Union of Bon:** south of Aurelia, the Bon Empire as it stands today. A few Bonnish islets lie off
  the Mugian coast to its west. See below.
- **Garaztia:** east of Aurelia-Anzaria and the Union of Bon. Never part of the Empire. See below.
- **Arrosia:** south of Garaztia and east of the Union of Bon. Never part of the Empire. See below.

## Ocidaria

The western continent, across the sea from Mugi, and Mugi's sphere of influence.

- ***Ukulwia:*** the northern coast, facing Mugi across the strait. Mugian since the colony was split.
  Its cotton feeds the Mugian mills.
- **The Umhlaba Dynasty:** the largest state, filling the west of the continent. Never part of the Empire,
  and the one power that could contest Mugi's hold on Ukulwia.
- **Ancania:** a small state almost surrounded by Umhlaba. Never part of the Empire.
- **Nouterria**, **Calidia** and **New Bon:** along the eastern coast, north to south. Former Imperial
  colonies, independent since they broke free from Bon.

## Orienzia

The eastern continent, and Arrosia's sphere of influence.

- ***The Northern Dominions:*** Arrosian settler colonies in the north, which stayed loyal when Lurberria
  rebelled.
- **Lurberria:** south of the Dominions, with an island off its north-east coast. Once Arrosia's richest
  colony, it won its independence in a revolt.
- ***The Iratic Holdings:*** islands off the western coast, owned by Arrosia's Iratic Company.
- **Haranlek**, **Iszavond**, **Ogrond** and **Dreplek:** the middle of the continent. Haranlek and
  Dreplek are related peoples.
- ***Unnamed Tribes:*** uncharted lands south of Dreplek.
- **The Zuiding** and **The Gamleat:** the south of the continent, two related peoples.

## Meridia

The southern continent, the one no power has claimed.

- **The Makan:** the great power of the continent, filling most of it.
- **Bhartunia** and **Saghiria:** on the Makan's western side. They and the Makan are related peoples.
- **The Seven Kingdoms:** on the Makan's eastern side, next to the Gamleat.

Further ***Unnamed Tribes*** hold an island in the south-west of the world and a smaller group of islands
in the central sea.

# Mugi

- West of Aurelia, bordering Ocidanten and, through South Arpal, North Arpal. Capital: **Anpurgi**.
- A **Tsardom-Electorate**, a descendant of the old imperial style: the nobility elect a Tsar for ten
  years at a time, where the Emperor of Bon is elected for life. Mugi has no parties, only
  noble houses and the families allied to them, competing over which house's head becomes Tsar.
- A colonial power with a strong navy, suzerain over **Ukulwia**. Ukulwia is held by a colonial army of
  mixed ranks, Mugian and Ukulwian, under Mugian officers. A Ukulwian can rise only to a lesser **native
  commission**, which runs a company but never commands a Mugian, and the guns are crewed by Mugians
  alone.
- **South Arpal**, Mugian since Mugi left the Empire, is the long arm of Mugian land running east along
  Aurelia's southern border, between Aurelia and the Union of Bon. Its countryside is Aurelian, but its
  capital, **Kolgasi**, is mostly Mugian.
- An exclave on the north-west coast of Anzaria, and island chains off Aurelia-Anzaria's north coast and in the
  strait towards Ukulwia.

# The Union of Bon

- The Bon Empire as reformed by the **Act of Union**: a patchwork of principalities, bishoprics and free
  cities bound by old law and a common **Diet**, each master of its own affairs.
- The **Emperor** is elected for life by the princes. He keeps the old title and ceremonies, but has no
  army of his own and no taxes beyond what the Diet grants.
- Prestige without power. The Union keeps the old treaties and is the natural judge of quarrels between the
  lands that left it, and the natural host of peace conferences, because it threatens no one.
- Some Bonnish princes owe money to Arrosian houses.

# Garaztia

- A centralised kingdom ruled by an absolute king, with no parliament. Its valleys and towns once kept
  their own laws and charters, until a line of strong kings swept them away for one law, one tax and one
  army, governed from the capital by royal officials. The valleys still remember their charters.
- The centralisation was paid for with loans from Arrosian merchant houses: a standing army, royal roads,
  harbours and paid officials. Garaztia is deep in Arrosian debt.
- Before the centralisation, Garaztian lords held estates in Provencie Gares, as borderland nobles held
  land on both sides. The crown stripped them of those estates, and some of their families now live in
  Gares as Aurelian subjects, with kin across the border.
- Relations with Aurelia-Anzaria are cool but civil. The current King of Garaztia is outgoing and well
  liked abroad.
- **Names** blend Spanish and Portuguese sounds, written without diacritics: King Fernao III, Baron Duarte
  Valcarcel.

# Arrosia

- A **merchant republic**, governed by a council of great merchant families. Only men of property vote,
  and the Republic's head is elected for a term.
- It was born from a bankruptcy. Arrosia was once a kingdom whose kings kept the Bon Empire out by
  borrowing from the merchant houses of the coastal cities, pledging the port tolls, then the customs, and
  at last the right to tax. When the last king died without an heir, his creditors held the treasury, the
  ports and the soldiers' pay, and crowned no one. Their agreement, the **Settlement**, is still the
  Republic's founding document: liberty to patriots, a debt agreement to cynics.
- The **Iratic Company**'s charter was one of the old crown's pledges, which is why the Company is almost
  a state within the state. It owns the Iratic Holdings, with its own ships and soldiers.
- The Republic still carries the old crown's debt. Taxing its colonies to pay it is what made **Lurberria**
  revolt. The colonists who stayed loyal fled north to the **Northern Dominions**, which the Republic now
  keeps loyal with trade privileges instead of taxes.
- Where Mugi is ruled by noble houses and admirals, Arrosia is ruled by merchants and its Company. It
  prefers a treaty to a war, and fights only when it is owed money.
- **Names** blend Basque and French sounds, written without diacritics: Sieur Benat Etcheverry,
  Colonel-Paymaster Gaston Iribarne.
- Having lost Lurberria, it looks for new trade to make up the loss, and Meridia is the one continent left
  unclaimed. Garaztia, between it and Aurelia-Anzaria, is its road north.

# Provinces of Aurelia

Aurelia's capital is **Aurelle**, in Aurenten. Aurelia borders Anzaria to the north, Mugi to the west and
south-west, the Union of Bon to the south and Garaztia to the east.

- **Mugi:** Ocidanten and North Arpal.
- **The Union of Bon:** Tovrerie, Iracterie, Soralenten and Silvicarie, and Agrinenten along a short
  stretch.
- **Garaztia:** Provencie Gares.

Only five provinces touch water:

- **The western bay:** Ocidanten, whose capital Lanten is Aurelia's great port.
- **The southern bay:** North Arpal and Tovrerie. Neither capital is a port.
- **The Great Lake:** Silvicarie and Provencie Gares. Neither capital is a port.
- **The north sea:** Provencie Gares, whose capital Escas is a port.

- **Aurenten** (capital **Aurelle**): the central heartland, entirely landlocked and surrounded by
  neighbouring provinces. Its high population density stems from fertile, well-irrigated river basins and
  rolling plains that support large-scale urban development and intense agricultural output.
- **Iracterie** (capital **Iracten**): east-central Aurelia, bordered by Aurenten, Agrinenten, Tovrerie,
  Soralenten and the Union of Bon. Landlocked yet heavily populated, its landscape consists of rich
  agricultural valleys, broad plains and heavy resource development.
- **North Arpal** (capital **Carden**): the southwestern frontier, sharing a long southern land border
  with Mugi's South Arpal and a coast on the southern bay. Key mountain passes, river valleys and strategic
  transit corridors connect internal Aurelia to its southern neighbours.
- **Agrinenten** (capital **Agrines**): the northern interior, the direct land bridge between central
  Aurelia and Anzaria, with a short border with the Union of Bon. Gently sloping plains and north-to-south
  river valleys carry overland movement between the two realms.
- **Ocidanten** (capital **Lanten**): the far western reach, wedged along the Mugian border. A large,
  continuous interior expanse of arable farmland, western hills and vital trade routes. Its coast on the
  western bay holds the port of Lanten and the smugglers' harbour of Saltmouth.
- **Soralenten** (capital **Sorales**): a long, narrow corridor in the northeast, bordered by Anzaria to
  the north and the Union of Bon to the south. A frontier zone of rugged uplands, border hills and narrow
  river valleys.
- **Silvicarie** (capital **Fidacren**): the eastern peninsula, bordered by the Union of Bon and the
  Great Lake to the south. Dense lakeside forests, rugged peninsular hills and sheltered inlets along its
  southern shore.
- **Provencie Sacs** (capital **Sagtias**): a compact, fully landlocked interior province in west-central
  Aurelia, surrounded entirely by other provinces. Sheltered inland plains and gentle terrain connect the
  western frontier to the central core.
- **Tovrerie** (capital **Tovres**): the southern tip of central Aurelia, on the southern bay and
  bordering the Union of Bon. Coastal lowlands, marshlands and rugged border foothills.
- **Provencie Gares** (capital **Escas**): the far eastern extremity, with the north sea to the north, the
  Great Lake to the south and Garaztia to the east. Thinly peopled, with windswept coastal cliffs, sea
  bluffs and isolated shores. Escas is its port on the north sea.
