# Provinces of Aurelia

Aurelia borders Anzaria to the north, a western nation, a southern realm and an eastern nation.
Only five provinces touch the sea: Ocidanten, North Arpal, Tovrerie, Silvicarie and Provencie Gares.

- **Aurenten:** the central heartland, entirely landlocked and surrounded by neighbouring provinces. Its
  high population density stems from fertile, well-irrigated river basins and rolling plains that support
  large-scale urban development and intense agricultural output.
- **Iracterie:** east-central Aurelia, bordered by Aurenten, Agrinenten, Tovrerie and Soralenten.
  Landlocked yet heavily populated, its landscape consists of rich agricultural valleys, broad plains and
  heavy resource development.
- **North Arpal:** the southwestern frontier, sharing a long southern land border with the neighbouring
  realm. Key mountain passes, river valleys and strategic transit corridors connect internal Aurelia to its
  southern neighbours.
- **Agrinenten:** the northern interior, the direct land bridge between central Aurelia and Anzaria.
  Gently sloping plains and north-to-south river valleys carry overland movement between the two realms.
- **Ocidanten:** the far western reach, wedged along the border of the western nation. A large,
  continuous interior expanse of arable farmland, western hills and vital trade routes. Its coast holds
  the port of Lanten and the smugglers' harbour of Saltmouth.
- **Soralenten:** a long, narrow corridor in the northeast, bordered by Anzaria to the north and the
  neighbouring territory to the south. A frontier zone of rugged uplands, border hills and narrow river
  valleys.
- **Silvicarie:** the eastern peninsula, bordered by a large southern bay. Dense coastal forests, rugged
  peninsular hills and sheltered inlets along its southern shore.
- **Provencie Sacs:** a compact, fully landlocked interior province in west-central Aurelia, surrounded
  entirely by other provinces. Sheltered inland plains and gentle terrain connect the western frontier to
  the central core.
- **Tovrerie:** the southern tip of central Aurelia, wrapping around the northern coast of the southern bay
  and bordering the southern realm. Coastal lowlands, marshlands and rugged border foothills.
- **Provencie Gares:** the far eastern extremity, exposed to the open sea to the north and east, and
  bordering the eastern nation to the south. Thinly peopled, with windswept coastal cliffs, sea bluffs and
  isolated maritime terrain.
